Every promise sits on a material system. We make that system visible.

The problem

Companies can measure targets. The harder question is what those targets are made of.

01 / PROMISE

Commitments are usually evaluated as language.

Targets, reports, and product stories can be clear while leaving the underlying system unexamined.

02 / MATTER

Products carry histories that claims do not.

Feedstocks, infrastructures, toxicities, labor, and geopolitical dependencies can contradict an intended future.

The method

A four-part analysis turns an abstract commitment into a decision-ready material map.

01

Parse the promise

Identify the claim, boundary, timeline, audience, and implied future.

02

Trace the matter

Map materials, energy, labor, infrastructure, waste, and historical dependencies.

03

Measure the gap

Score omissions, contradictions, uncertainties, and exposure across the system.

04

Rebuild the future

Translate findings into product decisions and a narrative the evidence can support.

The venture

Built at the intersection of material research, business, and future-making.

Promise & Matter is an early-stage venture concept founded by Lu Cheng, a PhD researcher in Science and Technology Studies at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. It translates humanities-based material analysis into a practical tool for corporate decision-making.

The first research domain is textiles, with a broader ambition: help any product company understand the futures its materials make possible—and the histories they continue to carry.
